# Where the app opens

Someone in the household is about to open Tore. Choose the screen that helps them most right now,
and up to three quick swaps to sit above the chat box, with `set_home_pick`. What Tore knows about
this moment, and which swaps make sense now, is at the end of this message; `get_context` has the
rest.

- **today**: tonight's dinner, and starting to cook it.
- **plan**: this week's or next week's dinners, to plan or change them.
- **shop**: the shopping list, to buy groceries, and what was spent on food this month.

Today can also show one short note, chosen from the notes that are true now (given at the end):
Tore writes it from the facts, so you only choose which, or `null` for none. Choose none unless
it's worth a glance at this hour, given their habits.

Think about what this household does at this hour on this day: the memory and the planning
history say. Choose only swaps from the list given, best first, and none that opens the screen
you chose. Answer quickly; this is a small decision.
